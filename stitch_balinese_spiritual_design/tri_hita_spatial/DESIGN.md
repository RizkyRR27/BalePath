## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the philosophy of Balinese sacred spatial order (*Tri Hita Karana* and *Sanga Mandala*), bridging timeless communal Hindu rituals with modern spatial GIS mobility. The aesthetic balance reflects reverent quietude and mission-critical civic transparency. It serves two distinct audiences: Balinese banjar administrators (*prajuru*, *kelian*, and *pecalang*) coordinating sacred road ceremonies, and multi-tier public commuters (daily commuters, delivery riders, emergency responders, and global travelers) navigating living ritual landscapes.

The visual style blends **Tactile Spiritual Heritage** with **Minimalist High-Function GIS**:
- **Sacred Architectural Grounding:** Tactile earthiness drawn from volcanic basalt (*batu candi*), warm carved terracotta brick (*bata merah Majapahit*), and weathered sandstone (*paras*), providing organic solidity.
- **Ritual Luminosity:** Golden tones derived from offerings (*canang sari* marigold petals, woven young coconut palm *janur*, and sacred gold gilding *emas prada*), illuminating actionable wayfinding cues and ceremony milestones.
- **Duality & Balance (*Rwa Bhineda*):** The iconic checkered rhythm of Balinese *Poleng* textiles (black, white, and sacred ash gray) is transmuted into structural borders, status chips, and spatial grid markers, symbolizing cosmic balance between mundane transport and sacred time.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Inspires reverence and community patience rather than congestion frustration, transforming traffic detours into moments of cultural appreciation and civic harmony.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in deep volcanic stone tones, natural clays, and ceremonial pigments, optimized for both daylight outdoor readability and nighttime navigation:

- **Primary (`#E5A93C` - Marigold & Janur Gold):** Serves as the primary focal point for key CTAs, active states, active road closures, and sacred ceremony alerts.
- **Secondary (`#C2593F` - Terracotta Candi):** Represents volcanic brickwork, emergency alerts, critical notices, and full roadblock boundaries.
- **Tertiary (`#3A7D6A` - Banyan Moss & Emerald Detour):** Denotes open bypass routes, verified clear detours, and environmental emission reduction metrics.
- **Neutral (`#1C1B1A` - Batu Candi Charcoal):** Deep volcanic stone serving as the core container background, creating high contrast against luminous gold and crisp sacred text.
- **Color Roles & System States:**
  - `closure-full` (`#D84A38`): Complete road blockade for high rites (*Ngaben*, *Melasti*, *Panca Yadnya*).
  - `closure-partial` (`#E5A93C`): Managed slowdown or alternating pass (*Buka-Tutup*) under Pecalang guidance.
  - `detour-safe` (`#2E8B57`): Recommended GIS transit paths for 2-wheel and 4-wheel vehicles.
  - `incense-smoke` (`#ECE5D8`) and `poleng-white` (`#F7F5F0`): Primary high-contrast content typography.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes sacred cultural depth with immediate navigational utility:

- **Headlines & Ceremony Titles (Noto Serif):** Evokes the engraved stone inscriptions (*prasasti*) and ceremonial manuscripts of temple courtyards. It bestows prestige and cultural reverence upon banjar names, ceremony headers, and cultural educational cards.
- **Body & Editorial (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A friendly, readable modern sans-serif designed for high legibility across quick-glance smartphone usage under glaring tropical sunlight.
- **Labels, GIS Metrics, & Status (Space Grotesk):** A precise geometric grotesque with technical personality. Used for coordinates, roadblock operational hours, Pecalang radio channel tags, carbon calculation metrics, and badge metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered around a full-viewport WebGIS foundation coupled with floating sheet interfaces:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Employs a split/docked architecture. The interactive map occupies 100% of the viewport, with a left-docked Banjar Dispatch panel (380px to 440px wide) featuring a 1.5rem internal gutter. Admin tools and AI extraction drawers utilize a sliding right-dock canvas.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Collapsible sidebar rail with a floating bottom drawer that expands to reveal ceremony schedules and detour summaries.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Fluid edge-to-edge spatial view with bottom sheet navigation. Critical controls (layer toggles, search, and emergency corridor alerts) float within safe thumb zones with a `1rem` outer margin from screen edges.
- **Rhythmic Baseline:** Built strictly upon an 8px grid foundation (`0.25rem` to `2.5rem`), ensuring consistent vertical cadence between cultural educational blurbs and time-stamped schedules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered stone surfaces, Poleng structural outlines, and subtle golden under-glows:

- **Base Layer (L0 - Spasial Ground):** Dark basalt map layer (`#121110`) with low-saturation roads and muted topography.
- **Level 1 (L1 - Embedded Panels & Base Cards):** `#1C1B1A` with a 1px structural stroke of `#34312E`.
- **Level 2 (L2 - Floating Sheets & Calendar Trays):** `#282624` accompanied by an ambient diffused shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)` with a faint warm terracotta tint (`rgba(194, 89, 63, 0.08)`).
- **Level 3 (L3 - Modal Dialogs, AI Event Parsers & Ceremony Popovers):** `#34312E` surface layered over a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(243, 195, 92, 0.25)` to evoke gilded temple shrines (*Pelinggih Prada*).
- **Poleng Accent Trims:** Instead of harsh dropshadows, critical alert surfaces feature a subtle 2px top border or tab accent echoing the balanced black/ash/white weave pattern.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Soft Architectural (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry:

- **Structural Containers:** Cards, panels, and modal containers use an intentional, low-radius border curve (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`). This mirrors the chisels of Balinese stonemasonry (*Paras Tarukan*) and tiered temple gate structures (*Candi Bentar*), avoiding bubbly consumer tech curves.
- **Pills for Dynamic Status:** Micro-tags, route badges (e.g., *Rute Alternatif*, *Buka-Tutup*), and Pecalang duty statuses retain soft pill forms (`9999px`) to immediately separate operational status chips from structural architectural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Emas Prada / Golden Action):** Solid `#E5A93C` fill, `#0A0A09` high-contrast bold typography (`label-md`), 0.25rem corner radius, with a subtle hover transition to `#F3C35C` and a warm 4px golden glow.
- **Secondary (Batu Candi Outline):** Semi-transparent `#1C1B1A` fill, 1px solid border of `#7D7871`, `#F7F5F0` text, transitioning on hover to a `#E5A93C` border.
- **Danger / Roadblock Total:** Solid `#D84A38` fill with `#F7F5F0` text, dedicated to total closure submissions and emergency rerouting triggers.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Compact 20px-high pills with a `Space Grotesk` font. Closed total routes use a dark red tint (`#D84A38` at 15% opacity with solid red border), partial closures use an amber marigold tint, and detours use moss green.
- **Poleng Tag:** Distinctive badge with a micro dual-square icon or checkered micro-border designating official Banjar Adat sanctioning.

### Cards & Cultural Sheets
- **Ceremony Roadside Card:** Built on `#1C1B1A` with a `#34312E` border. Header features a dual-tone layout: the ceremony name in `Noto Serif` (`#E5A93C`), accompanied by Pecalang in-charge badge, closure time window, and a dedicated expandable tab titled *"Makna Upacara"* (Cultural Meaning) to educate rerouted motorists.
- **AI Event Parser Box:** Input area designed like an artisan slate container. Prajuru banjar paste raw WhatsApp coordination messages, while an animated golden highlight identifies and tags detected dates, closure hours, and banjar territory.

### Form Inputs & Road Closure Drawers
- **Text & Segment Inputs:** Dark stone backdrop (`#121110`), 1px border of `#34312E`, focusing to `#E5A93C` with zero light leakage. Placeholder text uses muted ash (`#7D7871`).
- **Interactive Checkbox & Radios:** Angular 0.25rem boxes; active state fills with `#E5A93C` showcasing a checkmark in `#0A0A09`.

### Specialized GIS Map Markers
- **Ceremony Pin:** Teardrop beacon with a stylized Balinese *Janur* / flame emblem filled in marigold gold, pulsed by an ambient circular ripple for live ceremonies.
- **Pecalang Checkpoint Indicator:** Checkered black-and-white shield marking physical sentry locations, establishing immediate visual trust.
